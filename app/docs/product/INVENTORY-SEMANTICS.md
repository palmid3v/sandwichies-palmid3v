# Inventory Semantics — Sandwichies

## Purpose

Sandwichies now treats inventory as a transactional ledger instead of a display-only estimate. Product identity is centralized in Cupboard's shared `productCatalog`; "ingredient" is only the semantic role a product plays in Sandwichies recipes.

## Shared product identity

- All 44 exported Cupboard catalog entries receive one canonical identity.
- Home products use `HD-#######`; business products used by Sandwichies use `N-#######`.
- The 11 active recipe ingredients keep their semantic IDs (for example `pepperoni` and `mozzarella`) in Sandwichies and map to the corresponding canonical `N-` product IDs.
- Barcode is an optional product attribute, never the canonical identity.
- Cupboard owns the shared product catalog and separate Home/Business stock ledgers. Sandwichies never creates a second product identity or a separate authoritative stock balance.

## Opening baseline

The catalog field `stockQuantity` is a legacy recipe-costing baseline, not a physical stock balance or transaction history. For Cupboard-backed inventory, the real opening stock is the quantity registered in Cupboard's `Negocio · Sandwichies` workspace. Products with no exported physical balance are initialized at zero; recipe baselines are never converted into physical stock.

## Transaction types

### Purchase

Positive inventory movement recorded by an authenticated administrator through the trusted callable, applied to Cupboard, and mirrored locally using a stable operation ID so retrying does not add stock twice.

### Consumption

Negative inventory movement generated by the trusted backend when an order is confirmed. Confirmation fails if Cupboard cannot deduct every recipe ingredient atomically.

### Adjustment

Explicit positive or negative correction recorded by an administrator with a required reason, applied to Cupboard and mirrored locally with the same idempotent operation pattern.

## Completed-sale accounting

A completed sale is:

- order status = `entregado`;
- payment status = `pagado`.

Only completed sales contribute to the dashboard's sales, cost and profit reporting.

Sales reporting remains separate from stock reservation: only delivered and paid orders count as completed sales. On confirmation, quantities are deducted from Cupboard's `Negocio · Sandwichies` workspace; on cancellation after confirmation, the original quantities are restored exactly once. Cancelling a pending order does not change stock. Cupboard is the operational source of truth; Sandwichies mirrors its latest balances and movements for the admin view.

## Idempotency

The cross-project adapter uses deterministic movement identifiers based on order ID, ingredient ID and operation, so retries do not deduct or restore stock twice.

## Historical compatibility

Orders created before physical ingredient usage was stored may not contain enough information to reconstruct consumption safely. These orders are marked as unavailable for automatic inventory consumption rather than being guessed.

## Authorization

Administrators may read inventory balances and transaction history.

Browser writes to both inventory collections remain denied.

All inventory mutations are performed by trusted backend code.

## Operational model

The operational stock of record is Cupboard's business workspace. Sandwichies mirrors its latest Cupboard balances and movements; the mirror is not the authority for confirming an order.

Legacy/local balances use the catalog opening baseline plus recorded local transaction deltas. Cupboard-backed balances mirror the latest quantity and revision from Cupboard, including manual purchases and adjustments made through the Sandwichies admin callable.

The admin UI exposes:

- current balance;
- approximate servings based on portion quantity;
- recent movement history;
- purchase entry;
- stock adjustment entry.

Inventory does not yet model supplier invoices, batch/lot tracking, expiration dates, purchase prices by lot, or warehouse/location separation. Those are future extensions.
